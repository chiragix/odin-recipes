2. Lassi

3. Rabdi

Rabdi, also called Rabri, is a classic North Indian sweet made by slowly cooking milk until it thickens and reduces. As the milk cooks, layers of clotted cream (malai) are formed and collected, giving rabdi its signature texture. It is flavored with cardamom, saffron, nuts and dried fruits for a rich and satisfying dessert.
